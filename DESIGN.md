## Brand & Style
The brand personality is poetic, intimate, and steeped in sensory romance. Evoking the secret reverie of an overgrown botanical conservatory at dawn, the interface targets discerning gift givers, wedding celebrants, and curators of bespoke luxury experiences. Every interaction should feel like untying a heavy silk ribbon or turning the gilded vellum page of an heirloom love letter.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Romantic Editorial** with subtle **Ethereal Glassmorphism**. Dense, rich botanical depths of emerald green are illuminated by soft morning blush, glowing accents of polished brass, and petals steeped in velvety carmine. The visual presence rejects clinical digital minimalism in favor of ornate framing, atmospheric layered glows, and tactile physical craftsmanship.

## Colors
The palette captures the interplay between midnight garden foliage and morning light filtering across wet rose petals.

- **Primary (`#8A122A` - Velvety Carmine Rose):** The core romantic emotional anchor. Used for principal calls-to-action, active curation states, and delicate wax-seal style badges. Paired with an inner glow of `#B31B38` for tactile luminosity.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Polished Brass & Gold Foil):** Serves as the luxury finish. Applied to hairline borders, monograms, filigree dividers, rating stars, and subtle specular accents.
- **Tertiary (`#F4C2C2` - Morning Blush Light):** A warm, ethereal dawn tint applied to ambient lighting spots, soft tinted typography on dark surfaces, and subtle interactive highlights.
- **Neutral (`#081C15` - Deep Conservatory Emerald):** The foundational dark space. Provides profound botanical depth rather than stark digital black, allowing crimson and gilded golds to glow organically. Supported by container surface `#0E2A20` and elevated surface `#15392C`.

## Typography
Typography is treated with traditional French and Italian editorial gravity. High-contrast transitional serifs provide drama, while humanist old-style book types ensure effortless immersion.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Utilized for ceremonial headers, section names, and product titles. Display scale pairs italic emphasis with roman characters to recreate artisanal stationery typography.
- **Body & Labels (`EB Garamond`):** Offers organic warmth, generous x-height, and literary cadence. All metadata tags, category indicators, and uppercase buttons rely on `EB Garamond` in expanded tracking (12% to 15%) to evoke engraved typography.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs an editorial 12-column grid configured for deliberate, slow-paced visual storytelling. Columns breathe through wide 1.5rem gutters, allowing imagery of botanical foliage and gift presentations to command space.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width presentation container of 1320px with 3rem outer borders. Asymmetrical card pairings mimic coffee-table art monographs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins. Product cards transition into balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 1.25rem margins. Multi-column gift configurations reflow into single vertical stacks framed by gold hairline separators.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through ambient botanical layering rather than clinical drop shadows. 

1. **Surface Layers:**
   - **Ground Layer:** Midnight Emerald `#081C15`.
   - **Mid Layer (Cards, Gift Trays):** Semi-opaque `#0E2A20` tinted with 65% opacity and 16px background blur.
   - **Floating Layer (Modals, Hover Drawers):** `#15392C` bordered by a 1px brass filament line.

2. **Luminescence & Glow:**
   - Elevated cards carry an outer bloom: `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
   - Interactive primary buttons and ruby accents utilize an ethereal ruby aura: `0 0 20px 2px rgba(138, 18, 42, 0.45)`.
   - Structural divisions reject solid borders in favor of hairline strokes: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`.

## Shapes
A disciplined **Soft** curvature (`roundedness: 1`) is applied throughout the system to mimic archival deckled paper and bespoke stationery boxes:

- **Cards & Envelopes:** 0.25rem corner radius creates crisp, architectural tailoring reminiscent of fine gift boxing.
- **Interactive Badges & Wax Seals:** Elliptical or circular geometries are reserved exclusively for wax stamps, artisanal emblems, and flower-seal toggles.
- **Floating Controls & Modals:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem) softens overlaid gift customizers without surrendering traditional luxury rigor.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Wax Seals
- **Primary Action (Velvet Button):** Filled with `#8A122A`, framed with an inner inset highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(244, 194, 194, 0.3)`), and stamped with gold serif typography (`#F4C2C2` or `#FFFFFF`). Hover brings an ethereal ruby radiance and subtle scale lift.
- **Secondary Action (Brass Filament):** Transparent background, 1px border in `#D4AF37`, text styled in tracked capital `EB Garamond` in golden hues. Hover shifts background to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Ceremonial Seal (Wax Toggle):** Circular ruby button stamped with a gilded botanical crest, functioning as the signature interaction for "Seal & Send" or gift inspection.

### Presentation Cards & Trays
- Encased in translucent deep-emerald glass (`rgba(14, 42, 32, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- Bound by a continuous 1px gilded hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).
- Interior padding leverages `space-lg` to create expansive white space around gift imagery, accompanied by calligraphic rose watermark motifs in low opacity.

### Input Fields & Handwritten Inscriptions
- Text fields mimic custom stationery. Solid borders are replaced by an ivory/gold underline (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- Background remains a velvety translucent forest tint (`rgba(8, 28, 21, 0.5)`).
- Dedicated message card inputs default to italicized `Playfair Display` or expressive cursive display, conveying the authenticity of hand-penned ink.

### Selection Chips & Botanical Toggles
- Small capsules bordered in hairline gold foil.
- Active states flood with deep carmine crimson (`#8A122A`) and feature a minute glowing blush ember dot (`#F4C2C2`) beside the selection name.

### Checkboxes & Ribbon Radios
- Selection indicators are styled as petite brass florets or gilded squares with inner diamond checkmarks, avoiding generic system controls.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Gift Unfolding Ribbon Container:** An animated presentation card that visually "unwraps" via soft easing, revealing the inscribed card against an emerald velvet backdrop.
- **Petal Shimmer Particle Field:** A delicate, ambient background overlay rendering drifting luminous rose petals and soft bokeh morning light.
